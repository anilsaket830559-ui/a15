about blouson

A jacket with a relaxed point of view.

Blouson is an independent fashion website about one easy-going jacket.

We love the blouson for its comfort and its history, and we think it is due a comeback. Our guides explain the details, proportions and colours that make it work, and our tools help you picture an outfit.

We do not sell clothes, take payment for mentions or use affiliate links. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
