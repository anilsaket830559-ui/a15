Side A

What Is a Blouson?

The name comes from the French for a loose, blousy top. The jacket keeps the idea: a roomy body pulled in at the waist so it billows gently above the hem.

Track 1

The defining detail

A blouson is gathered at the hem with elastic, a drawcord or a rib band, so the fuller body puffs above it. Cuffs are usually gathered too. Collars vary: shirt, stand or funnel.

Track 2

Blouson or bomber?

BlousonBomber
BodyRoomy, billowingFitted to slightly full
HemGathered, often elastic or drawcordRibbed knit band
CollarShirt, stand or funnelRib knit or fur

Every bomber is a kind of blouson, but not every blouson is a bomber.

Track 3

Where it came from

Waist-gathered jackets appeared in work and military wear in the early twentieth century, because a closed hem keeps out wind and stops the jacket catching. Designers later took the shape into everyday fashion, and it peaked in the relaxed silhouettes of the 1970s and 1980s.

Track 4

Fabrics

  • Nylon and polyester: light, wind-resistant, packable.
  • Cotton twill and gabardine: smart, breathable, for spring.
  • Suede and leather: luxurious and warm; heavier.
  • Wool: cosy, for cold, dry days.
Track 5

Fit to look for

Shoulders should still sit at your shoulder edge; the roominess belongs in the body, not the shoulders. The gathered hem should rest at the waist or hips, and you should be able to lift your arms without the hem riding up.
