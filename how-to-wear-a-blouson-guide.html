How to Wear a Blouson

Volume on top needs a little balance below. Get the proportions right and a blouson is one of the easiest jackets you can own.

  1. 1. Balance the volume
  2. 2. Mind the length
  3. 3. Three easy outfits
  4. 4. Play with colour
  5. 5. Care
Track 1

Balance the volume

Pair a roomy blouson with straight or slightly tapered trousers so the outfit does not look heavy all over. If you love wide trousers, choose a cropped, lighter blouson.

Track 2

Mind the length

Waist length suits most people and shows the gathered hem best. Cropped styles look great with high-waisted trousers; hip-length styles are relaxed and cover a longer top.

Track 3

Three easy outfits

WeekendT-shirt, straight jeans, trainers.
Smart casualKnit polo or shirt, chinos, loafers.
RetroPatterned shirt, wide cords, chunky boots.
Track 4

Play with colour

Earthy rust, mustard, olive and teal give a warm, retro feel. Navy and stone are the most versatile. Use the colour matcher to plan the rest of the outfit.

Track 5

Care

Check the label: many nylon and cotton blousons can be washed cool and line-dried. Leather and suede need specialist care. Store on a wide hanger so the shoulders keep their shape and the elastic is not stretched.
